I'm a **Big Data Management Master's student** at the University of Warsaw and a **Global Data & Analytics Intern at Danone**, where I work on automation, Power BI dashboards, and SQL-based reporting.

My background is a bit unusual — I started with a **Management degree**, did an **Erasmus in Barcelona**, and gradually moved into the world of data. Today I combine business thinking with technical skills, which I believe is a real advantage in data science.

I'm currently growing my skills in **Python, machine learning, and statistical modelling** — and I love projects where data actually makes a difference.
